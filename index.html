FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Useful answers before your project begins.

Do you tint both vehicles and buildings?

Yes. Autotrix offers window tinting for vehicles and building windows. Tell us about your vehicle or space so we can discuss suitable film options.

How long does window tinting take?

Timing depends on the vehicle or building, the amount of glass, existing film and the installation requirements. Contact us with your project details for a schedule and care instructions.

Which window film should I choose?

The right film depends on your glass, your priorities and the installation. We can discuss comfort, glare, appearance and privacy before you decide. Film performance and visibility vary by product and lighting.

Can you help with the design for my sign?

Yes. We can work with your existing artwork or help develop a design for custom signs and graphics. Share your logo, approximate dimensions and where the sign will be installed to get started.

Do you offer full color-change vehicle wraps?

We no longer offer full color-change wraps. We continue to offer commercial vehicle wraps, business lettering and branded vehicle graphics.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

Start with the service you need, approximate dimensions or vehicle details, your location and preferred timing. Photos and existing artwork can be shared when you email us.
